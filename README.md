

Simble bot with 2 motored wheels and one back free wheel.
It ha the distance sensor, and motored pusher in front.

BOM:
* Controller: Arduino Board model UNO R3
* Motors shield: L293D MH Electronics board for 4 motors and 2 servos
* Distance sensor: HC-SR04
* Servo: SG90 4.8-6V 180/Micro Servo
* Motors: 3xLEGO L-motors
* Wires: 4 LEGO PF system vires with open ends
* Power: 6 pics 1.5 V AA battaries (LEGO battareis box)
